01

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data required to create customized project estimates, manage project milestones, and provide authenticated access to client portals:

  • Account & Profile Credentials: When you register or log in, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number for authentication, session recovery, and account identification.
  • Google OAuth Data: When utilizing Google Sign-In, we receive your public profile name, email address, and profile picture avatar via secure Google Identity Services tokens.
  • Project & Quotation Requests: Project titles, category requirements, duration timelines, scope descriptions, and milestone budget preferences you configure inside the Quotation Builder.
  • File Attachments: Design files, wireframes, PDFs, or specification briefs you choose to upload alongside your quotation requests.
  • Invoicing & Transaction Milestones: Records of advance payments, pending invoice balances, and milestone completion statuses. (Note: No sensitive payment card numbers or banking PINs are processed or stored on our servers).
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data: Anonymized app performance metrics, OS release, device model, and crash logs to maintain app stability.

02

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • To compute project quotation estimates and transmit client proposals to development teams.
  • To track real-time project progress, milestone deliveries, and phase activations.
  • To synchronize invoice statuses, advance receipts, and client financial dashboards.
  • To authenticate accounts securely and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To provide notifications on quote reviews, status approvals, and project updates.
🔒 Core Principle

We never monetize, harvest, rent, or sell your personal or project data to data brokers, ad networks, or third-party marketers.

03

Device Permissions Usage

The BYOB mobile application declares only the minimal set of Android runtime permissions required for its feature set:

  • Storage / Media Documents (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Media Picker): Used exclusively when you choose to attach specification documents, briefs, or image wireframes to your project quotation request.
  • Network Connectivity (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to communicate with our cloud database pipeline, verify active sessions, and synchronize project milestones.
  • Vibration Feedback (VIBRATE): Enhances usability through subtle haptic confirmation upon successful quote submission and action triggers.

Note: You can grant or revoke file permissions at any time through your Android Device Settings > Apps > BYOB > Permissions.

04

Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal and confidential project assets:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): All network traffic between your device and the cloud API is encrypted end-to-end.
  • Enterprise Database Architecture: Data is persisted in PostgreSQL instances with strict role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Local Cache Protection: Active session tokens stored on your device leverage encrypted operating system secure storage.
  • Isolated Client Scopes: Customers can only query and retrieve project records, quotes, and invoices associated with their verified account credentials.
05

Third-Party Infrastructure & SDKs

BYOB integrates select trusted third-party providers strictly for infrastructure and authentication:

  • Google Identity & OAuth Services: Facilitates secure, one-tap account authentication (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Expo & React Native Framework: Modern mobile runtime environment and native bridge libraries (Expo Privacy Policy).

No third-party analytics or behavioral ad trackers are embedded in the BYOB production release builds.

06

User Rights & Account Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, GDPR, and global data privacy standards, you have the full right to:

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of all quotations, project records, and contact details linked to your account.
  • Rectification: Correct or update your name, email, or telephone number at any time via in-app profile settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Request the permanent deletion of your account and associated project history from our systems.

To request complete account or project data erasure, email us at rajagopal333777@gmail.com with the subject line [BYOB Account Deletion Request]. Requests are processed within 7 business days.
