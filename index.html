SEC 01

1. Introduction

Welcome to BYOB (Bring Your Own Budget). BYOB is a project-based platform that enables individuals, startups, businesses, entrepreneurs, and organisations to submit their project requirements along with their proposed budgets.

The platform supports a wide range of projects, including software development, hardware development, electronics, embedded systems, IoT, artificial intelligence, automation, product design, research and development, and other technology-driven solutions.

At BYOB, we understand that your project ideas, business plans, technical documents, product designs, and personal information are valuable. This Privacy Policy explains how BYOB collects, uses, stores, processes, shares, and protects the information submitted through our website, application, and associated services.

Acceptance of Policy: By accessing or using BYOB, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
SEC 02

2. Information We Collect

To understand your project requirements and provide appropriate solutions, we may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal Information

When submitting a project request or creating an account, users may be required to provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Company or organisation name, if applicable
  • Location, city, and country
  • Designation or professional details, where applicable

2.2 Project Information

To understand and evaluate your requirements, we may collect:

  • Project title and description
  • Proposed project budget
  • Project category: Software, Hardware, AI, IoT, Electronics, etc.
  • Functional and technical requirements
  • Expected project outcomes
  • Preferred project timeline
  • Development and deployment requirements
  • Additional information provided through project submission forms

2.3 Uploaded Documents and Files

BYOB allows users to upload supporting documents to explain their project ideas and requirements. These documents may include:

  • PDF and Word documents
  • Images and reference photographs
  • Product designs and technical drawings
  • CAD files and engineering diagrams
  • Source code and software documentation
  • Excel sheets and project reports
  • Videos and presentations
  • Research papers and reference materials
  • Business plans, product concepts, and specifications
  • Other files relevant to the submitted project

We process these files primarily to understand your project, assess its feasibility, estimate development costs, and prepare suitable project proposals.

Important Notice: Users should only upload documents and information that they are authorised to share with BYOB.

2.4 Technical and Usage Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Website access logs
  • Application usage information
  • Session information and cookies
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

This information helps us maintain platform security, improve performance, and identify technical issues.

SEC 03

3. How We Use Your Information

BYOB collects and processes information for the following specific purposes:

  1. To understand the user's project idea and requirements.
  2. To evaluate the feasibility of the proposed project.
  3. To assess the proposed budget against the project scope.
  4. To identify the technical resources, components, and expertise required.
  5. To prepare quotations, cost estimates, and project proposals.
  6. To communicate with users regarding their submissions.
  7. To request additional information or clarification.
  8. To execute approved projects and provide associated services.
  9. To provide technical support, maintenance, and deployment assistance.
  10. To maintain internal project records and transaction history.
  11. To prevent fraudulent activities, misuse, and unauthorised access.
  12. To improve our platform, services, and user experience.
  13. To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or permission where required.

SEC 04

4. Project Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

We recognise that project submissions may contain confidential business information, innovative product concepts, technical specifications, unpublished research, and intellectual property. BYOB follows these principles:

  • Submitted project documents are intended for internal project evaluation and service delivery.
  • Access to submitted documents will be limited to authorised personnel and individuals who need the information to evaluate, manage, or execute the project.
  • We will not intentionally publish or publicly display your submitted project documents, designs, or confidential technical information without your permission.
  • We will not sell your uploaded project files or confidential project information to third parties.
  • We will not use confidential project submissions for unrelated commercial purposes without appropriate authorisation.

Intellectual Property Ownership

Submitting a project idea, document, design, or technical specification does not automatically transfer ownership of your intellectual property to BYOB. Unless otherwise agreed through a separate written agreement:

  • Users retain ownership of intellectual property they own and submit to the platform.
  • BYOB receives limited permission to access and use submitted materials for evaluating, discussing, quoting, and delivering the requested services.
  • Ownership of newly developed software, hardware designs, inventions, source code, prototypes, and other deliverables will be governed by the applicable project agreement or contract.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights to upload and share the materials they submit.

Confidentiality Note & Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA): This Privacy Policy is not a substitute for a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Users who require additional contractual confidentiality protection may request an NDA before sharing particularly sensitive technical or commercial information.
SEC 05

5. Sharing of Information

BYOB does not sell or rent users' personal information or submitted project documents. However, information may be shared in limited circumstances:

5.1 Internal Project Teams

Information may be shared with authorised employees, developers, engineers, designers, project managers, and other team members responsible for evaluating or executing the project.

5.2 External Service Providers

We may engage trusted third-party service providers for:

  • Cloud hosting and storage
  • Website and application infrastructure
  • Communication and email services
  • Payment processing
  • Security and monitoring
  • Technical support and maintenance

Such providers may process information on our behalf where necessary to provide their services.

5.3 Project Execution Partners

Where a project requires specialised expertise, manufacturing facilities, hardware components, external consultants, or other implementation partners, relevant project information may be shared when necessary. Where appropriate, confidentiality obligations will be established before sharing sensitive project information.

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, court orders, government authorities, or other legally binding obligations.

5.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business operations, relevant information may be transferred subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

SEC 06

6. Data Storage and Security

BYOB takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information submitted through its platform. Depending on the nature of the information and the services used, security measures may include:

  • Secure data transmission using HTTPS/SSL.
  • Access restrictions and authentication mechanisms.
  • Controlled access to uploaded documents.
  • Secure cloud storage arrangements.
  • Regular security monitoring.
  • Backup and recovery mechanisms.
  • Protection against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect user information, no internet-based platform or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged not to share passwords, authentication credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through project submissions.

SEC 07

7. Data Retention and Deletion

BYOB retains personal information and project-related documents only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Evaluating submitted project requests.
  • Preparing quotations and proposals.
  • Executing ongoing projects.
  • Providing maintenance and support.
  • Maintaining business and financial records.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.
  • Resolving disputes and enforcing agreements.

Where a project submission is not accepted, does not proceed, or is abandoned, associated information may be retained for a reasonable period for follow-up, administrative, and business purposes.

Users may request deletion of their personal information and uploaded project documents by contacting our support team at Skaiquanttechnologies@gmail.com. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate record-keeping obligations, or to resolve existing disputes. Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

SEC 08

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

BYOB may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Improve website functionality.
  • Analyse website traffic and usage.
  • Enhance platform security.
  • Understand and improve user experience.

Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform. Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent for non-essential cookies and tracking technologies.

SEC 09

9. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable laws, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Withdraw consent previously provided for data processing.
  • Request information regarding the processing of their personal data.
  • Raise complaints regarding privacy-related concerns.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise applicable rights where legally permitted.

Users may submit privacy-related requests through our designated contact email. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable laws and reasonable verification procedures.

BYOB is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party platforms that we do not control. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of external services before sharing information with them.

SEC 11

BYOB is intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old or otherwise legally authorised to enter into applicable agreements.

We do not knowingly encourage children to submit personal information or project documents without appropriate parental or guardian involvement and legally required consent. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.
